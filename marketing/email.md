A short note, because some people in a job search also take on contract or freelance work, either to bridge the gap or because they're considering it long term.

If that's you, the first question is usually "what do I charge?", and most people answer it too low. Here's a quick way to avoid that:

1. Start with what you'd want to keep per year, after tax.
2. Add tax, and the costs an employer used to cover: software, equipment, insurance, health cover, retirement.
3. Divide by the hours clients will actually pay for, not the hours you'll work. Finding clients and admin take real time. Plan on billing 50% to 65% of your hours.

That last step is the one people skip, and it's often the biggest difference.
