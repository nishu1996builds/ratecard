# Ratecard: The Pricing Studio for Freelancers and Consultants

**Hook:** Stop pricing from fear. Find your real floor rate, price on value, and send a three-tier proposal clients actually say yes to.

---

## Description

Most freelancers undercharge for one reason: they work out a rate from their costs, then knock a bit off so it "feels safe." Clients don't buy your costs. They buy outcomes. Ratecard is a private, offline pricing studio that walks you from the number you can never go below to the number the work is actually worth, and then helps you send it.

It runs as a single file in your browser. No account, no subscription, no data leaving your machine.

### What it does for you

- **Know your true floor in two minutes.** Enter the income you want to keep, your tax rate, expenses, time off, and realistic billable utilization. Ratecard gives you a minimum hourly rate, a day rate, and a monthly revenue target, with a clear chart of where every invoice actually goes.
- **See the utilization trap before it hits you.** A live curve shows how your floor climbs when billable time drops. Most rate calculators quietly assume you bill every hour you work. This one doesn't.
- **Price on value with confidence.** Estimate the revenue gained, costs saved, and risk reduced for your client, discount it honestly for uncertainty, and get a recommended fee range plus the ROI multiple the client gets at every price point. You also see exactly how much more value pricing earns you than billing hours.
- **Build a Good / Better / Best proposal in minutes.** Editable packages, deliverables, timelines, and prices, with a live anchoring check that tells you when your tiers are working against you. Prints to a clean, professional PDF in one click.
- **Turn scope creep into paid work.** Enter the extra request and your estimate, choose a rush level, and get a polite, ready-to-send change order message. One click copies it to your clipboard.
- **Learn the moves that matter.** A built-in playbook of ten tactics, with word-for-word scripts for raising rates on existing clients, answering "that's too expensive," and trading scope instead of discounting.

### Who it's for

- Freelance designers, developers, writers, and marketers who suspect they're undercharging
- Independent consultants moving from hourly billing to fixed or value-based fees
- Agency owners and studios who want a fast, consistent way to package and quote work
- Anyone about to send a proposal and wondering if the number is right

### What's included

- Ratecard app (a single HTML file that works offline in any modern browser, on desktop or mobile)
- Five modules: Floor Rate, Value Pricing, Three-Tier Proposal Builder, Change Order Generator, Pricing Playbook
- Six currencies: USD, EUR, GBP, INR, CAD, AUD
- Light and dark mode
- Print-ready proposal layout for saving as PDF
- Free updates to future versions

Your inputs save automatically in your browser, so it's ready where you left off next time.
